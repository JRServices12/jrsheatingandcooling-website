If you rent out a property with gas appliances, the law requires you to have them checked every year. With so many rented homes across Greenwich, Woolwich, Bexley, Dartford and Gravesend, this is one of the most common jobs we do. Here's what's involved.

## What is a landlord gas safety certificate?

It's the record of an annual gas safety check, carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer. You'll often hear it called a **CP12** or a **Landlord Gas Safety Record**. The requirement comes from the Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998.

## What the law asks of landlords

- **Every 12 months,** each gas appliance and flue you provide must be checked by a Gas Safe registered engineer.
- **Give your tenants a copy** within 28 days of the check.
- **New tenants** must get a copy before they move in.
- **Keep your records** for at least two years.
- **Keep everything maintained** between checks, not just tested once a year.

You can have the check done up to two months before the deadline and still keep the original expiry date, so you don't lose any time by booking early.

## What gets checked

- Gas appliances you own and supply, such as the boiler, gas hob or oven, and gas fire
- Flues and chimneys, to make sure fumes are being carried away safely
- Gas pressure and how well each appliance is burning
- Ventilation
- Safety devices working properly
- Pipework tightness

Appliances that belong to your tenant aren't your responsibility, but the pipework that serves them is.

## Carbon monoxide and smoke alarms

Since October 2022, landlords in England must fit a carbon monoxide alarm in any room with a fixed combustion appliance (apart from gas cookers), and a smoke alarm on every floor. We'll test any CO alarms near the gas appliances while we're there.
