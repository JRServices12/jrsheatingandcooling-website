A cold house is stressful, especially on the first cold morning of the year. Before you book a call-out, these quick checks are worth a try. None of them involve opening up the boiler.

> **If you smell gas, don't do any of these.** Open the windows, don't use electrical switches or naked flames, turn off the gas at the meter if you can, and call the National Gas Emergency line on **0800 111 999**.

## 1. Is there power to the boiler?

Check the boiler display is on. If it's blank, check the fused spur switch next to it and your fuse box for a tripped switch.

## 2. Check the boiler pressure

Low pressure is one of the most common reasons a boiler stops. With the heating cold, it should usually read about 1 to 1.5 bar. [Here's how to check and top it up](/blog/boiler-losing-pressure/).

## 3. Check the thermostat and timer

- Turn the room thermostat up a few degrees above the current temperature.
- Check the timer or programmer is calling for heating or hot water, and the clock is right. Power cuts and the clocks changing often reset them.
- If your thermostat is wireless, try fresh batteries.

## 4. Is there a gas supply?

Check your gas hob lights, if you have one. If it doesn't, the problem may be with the supply or a prepayment meter running out, rather than the boiler.

## 5. Look for a frozen condensate pipe

In freezing weather, the plastic condensate pipe that runs outside from modern boilers can freeze and make the boiler lock out. You might hear gurgling. Pouring **warm, not boiling,** water over the pipe outside can thaw it, then reset the boiler.

## 6. Reset the boiler once

Most boilers have a reset button. Press it **once**, as your manual describes. If it locks out again, don't keep resetting it: note the error code and call an engineer.

## When to call a Gas Safe engineer

- The boiler keeps locking out or showing the same error code
- Water is leaking from the boiler
- You hear loud banging or the boiler is making unusual noises
- The flame is yellow or orange instead of blue, or there are black marks around the boiler
