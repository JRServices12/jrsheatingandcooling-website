If you're replacing a boiler, the first decision is which type to go for. There's no single "best" boiler. The right one depends on your home, how many bathrooms you have and how many people use hot water at once.

## Combi boiler

A combi heats water straight from the mains as you use it, so there's **no hot water cylinder and no tanks in the loft**.

**Good for:** flats and small to medium houses with one bathroom, which covers a lot of homes across South East London.

- Saves space, as you lose the cylinder cupboard and loft tanks
- Hot water on demand, so you never run out
- Flow can drop if two taps or showers are running at once
- Needs decent mains water pressure

## System boiler

A system boiler heats water that's **stored in a cylinder**, but the main parts are built into the boiler, so there are **no tanks in the loft**.

**Good for:** homes with two or more bathrooms, or where several people need hot water at the same time.

- Strong flow to more than one shower or tap at once
- Needs room for a hot water cylinder
- Hot water can run out if the cylinder is used up, until it reheats

## Conventional boiler

Also called a regular or heat-only boiler. It uses a **hot water cylinder plus a cold water tank in the loft**.

**Good for:** older homes that already have this setup, especially where mains water pressure is low.

- Often the simplest swap if your system already works this way
- Takes up the most space
- Water pressure depends on how high the loft tank is

## Can I switch to a combi?

Often, yes. Changing from a tank in the loft to a combi is one of the most common upgrades we do. It frees up the airing cupboard and the loft. It usually takes longer than a straight swap, because the pipework has to change, and it's only a good idea if a combi can supply your home properly.

## Which brand?

We install and service Worcester Bosch, Vaillant, Ideal and Glow-worm. Each makes a good range, so the choice usually comes down to the right model and size for your home rather than the badge.

When we quote for a new boiler, we'll look at your home, your water pressure and how you use hot water, then recommend the right type and size.
